## Known limits

- **No per-row provenance** (#179, owner ruling 2026-10-19, no relabelling), and 85% of rows cannot even be joined
  to a stamped oracle output (above).
- **Shorter than production:** training median 900 chars vs production 2,235 (above), and positives concentrate in
  long text. The student sees short negatives far more often than production does.
- **Populations the labelling process cannot emit:**
  - nothing under 300 chars (#93; the generated block confirms it);
  - no `news.google.com` rows in the easy-negative draw (FM-S2, `docs/evidence/2026-10-07-belonging-adj1-build/`);
    Google News coverage elsewhere is unknown (generated block);
  - nothing from a source NexusMind excludes upstream.
- **Labels are judge-relative.** Which rows were demoted, which became hard negatives and which harvest rows became
  positives were decided by Claude judges; only the owner's 14 spot-check rows are independent.
- **Single-person stories are unresolved, by design.** c2b keeps no `out_one_moment` negatives, and live v3 misses
  the owner's top-of-page bar (`STATUS.md`, `docs/TODO.md` item 0a). This set does not encode that bar.
- **Positives are rare:** 3.7–4.2% per split at 4.0, so any recall figure on `test` rests on 29 rows. The gate used
  held-out set 3 instead (`STATUS.md` § *Evidence*).

## Intended use, and not for

- **For:** training and calibrating `belonging v3`'s student at the 4.0 op-point.
- **Not for:**
  - its deploy gate: that was held-out set 3, judged separately (now spent);
  - ground truth of any other lens (ADR-015: lenses overlap by design);
  - any evaluation of the oracle itself: these are oracle labels, partly reshaped by Claude judges;
  - training a model outside this repo's filters.

## Terms of the source text and of the labels

- **Source articles.** Publisher text, used as training data under the 2026-08-05 TDM decision
  (`docs/decisions/2026-08-05-tdm-opt-out-training-data.md`). Its open carve-out applies: the oracle ships the
  article text to the vendor, a risk the owner accepted. This split records no source per row beyond the id prefix,
  so no per-source licence list can be generated from it.
- **Labels, from the oracle.** Gemini's terms on training a model from its outputs were not read. The owner
  ACCEPTED that risk on 2026-10-21 (#174, closed; recorded beside the TDM decision).
- **Labels, from Claude.** 187 demotions, the 135 hard negatives' class and the selection of the 236 harvest
  positives come from Claude Opus 5.5 subagent output on the owner's subscription. Which Anthropic terms govern that
  was not read. The owner ACCEPTED that risk on 2026-10-21: the #174 ruling's scope was extended the same day to every past
  Claude label inside a trained filter (`docs/decisions/2026-08-05-tdm-opt-out-training-data.md` § *Scope extended
  2026-10-21*).
