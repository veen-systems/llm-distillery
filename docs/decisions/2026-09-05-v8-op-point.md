# human_thriving v8 — the phase-8 operating point: 4.5 stands, on the calibrated scale

**Owner ruling, 2026-09-05.** Phase 8's only decision. `EXP-017` / `EXP-025`;
evidence `docs/evidence/2026-09-04-v8-probe-calibration/PHASE_C_REVIEW.md`.

## The ruling

**4.50, on the CALIBRATED scale.** No code changes: the number the runtime already
carries is the number that was chosen. What changes is its status — it was *inherited
from uplifting v7* (#102, 2026-08-10) and is now **re-derived on v8's own held-out
split and ratified**.

## Why — the shape of the trade, not the level

Held-out, calibrated arm, 87 junk + 30 agreed-good rows (the rows `uplifting v7`
surfaced, partitioned by what the v8 oracle says about them):

| calibrated bar | junk removed | good kept | spec | step from the bar above |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.00 | 66.7% | 27/30 | 0.9264 | — |
| 3.50 | 79.3% | 24/30 | 0.9568 | **−3 good, −11 junk** |
| 3.75 | 82.8% | 22/30 | 0.9664 | −2 good, −3 junk |
| 4.00 | 87.4% | 18/30 | 0.9760 | −4 good, −4 junk |
| 4.25 | 89.7% | 16/30 | 0.9840 | −2 good, −2 junk |
| **4.50 (chosen)** | **94.3%** | **12/30** | **0.9920** | −4 good, −4 junk |
| 5.00 | 98.9% | 3/30 | 0.9984 | −9 good, −4 junk |

⭐ **From 3.75 upward every step costs almost exactly ONE agreed-good article per junk
article removed.** That is the whole argument. ADR-023 breaks a 1:1 trade toward
specificity — *"letting junk through is way worse than not catching positives; junk kills
readers, positives they don't know about don't hurt them"* — so the strictest reachable
bar wins, and 4.50 is it.

⚠️ **The frontier bends at 3.50**, where −3 good buys −11 junk. Anyone who later wants
materially more volume should move THERE, not to 4.00 or 4.25: the intermediate bars buy
volume at exactly par, so they are the worst place on the curve to spend.

## What the design weighting does, and does not, change

The 660-row split is drawn under a **25.1×** stratified design. Weighted (Horvitz–Thompson,
`phase_c_outcome.py`), 4.50 calibrated reads **95.0% junk removed / 40.2% good kept**
against the unweighted 94.3% / 40.0%. Over all 7 bars and **both** arms the maxima are
**+2.44 pp** junk-removed and **+0.20 pp** good-kept, so the trade has the same shape on
either arm and **4.50 does not become a different decision when weighted**.

⛔ **But those are the two quantities that move LEAST**, and the record should not imply
otherwise. Specificity — ADR-023's own criterion — moves up to **+2.65 pp** and recall
**−8.51 pp**; the v8 positive rate goes **5.3030% → 3.1638%**. The trade columns are
conditional on the v7-surfaced partition, which is why they are the stabler instrument for
*this* decision and why quoting only them understates the weighting elsewhere.

## Three things verified rather than assumed

1. ⛔ **The score the op-point meets IS the calibrated one**, established from the code
   path: `filters/common/filter_base_scorer.py:315-317` applies `calibration.json` to the
   raw logits, `:326` computes `weighted_avg` from the calibrated dimensions, and `:340`
   hands that value to `_assign_tier`. v8 ships a `calibration.json`, so the runtime
   comparison is calibrated-vs-4.5. Had this gone the other way the ruling would need a
   different number: **4.5 calibrated is a stricter bar than 4.5 raw** — 17 test rows
   flagged where raw flags 26.
2. **Executed at the boundary**, not read off the config: `_assign_tier(4.4999)` → `low`,
   `_assign_tier(4.5000)` → `medium`.
3. **All four places agree.** `TIER_THRESHOLDS` 4.5 (the runtime one); `config.yaml
   scoring.tiers.medium.threshold` 4.5 (documentation); `tests/unit/
   test_normalization_op_point.py` parametrized at 4.5; and `normalization.json`
   **correctly does not exist yet** — it is fitted at Phase E from production data, and
   `stats.raw_min` must equal 4.5 when it is.

## The constraint that bounded the choice

⛔ **5.00 was never available.** `scripts/normalization/fit_normalization.py:61` sets
`MAX_NORMALIZATION_RAW_MIN = 4.5` (NM#205), the fitter refuses an anchor above it, and the
production loader silently falls back to `score_scale_factor` for a `normalization.json`
that breaches it. **4.5 is accepted with ZERO margin** (strict `>`), so this op-point
cannot rise later without raising that constant in both repos.

## What it costs, stated plainly

v8 surfaces **17 of 660** held-out rows — 12 true positives, 5 false — at 4.50 calibrated.
Recall 0.343, specificity 0.9920. **The low recall is the cheap error, deliberately
chosen.** ⛔ Never set that 0.343 beside the fleet's 0.59–0.72: v7 and v8 do not share a
positive class (Jaccard 0.246 on the same 660 rows), so those are two different quantities
with one name.

## Owed at Phase 9, not now

⚠️ **NexusMind's enrichment gate** (`pipeline.enrichment.min_score`, 4.0) reads the
**normalized** score, not this one (NM#319). Raising an op-point cannot starve it; a filter
whose *distribution* sits low can. Check v8's normalized CDF against it when Phase E fits
`normalization.json` — this ruling does not settle it.
