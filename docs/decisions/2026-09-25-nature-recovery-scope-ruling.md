# Nature recovery scope ruling — completed steps count (2026-09-25)

**Owner ruling, given in session**, after the Nature recovery miss audit showed the owner's concept of the
lens is broader than nature_recovery v4's prompt (`docs/evidence/2026-09-25-nature-recovery-miss-audit/`).
The owner chose between options the assistant wrote; the ruling is the option selected, in the wording
below. The owner first ticked all four categories IN, asked *"I may be too generous, what do you think?"*,
heard the assistant's recommendation and then chose this option.

## NR-1. Completed restoration or protection steps are IN, even before recovery is measured

> Completed restoration/protection actions count even before recovery is measured; plans, short-lived
> gains and research without outcomes stay out.

- **IN:** an action that has already happened and removes pressure or restores habitat. Examples from the
  audit: native meadow planting carried out; farmers who have stopped burning stubble for six years; a
  country ratifying a treaty that is already in force.
- **OUT (unchanged from v4):** plans and early-stage intentions (a reintroduction "at a very early stage"),
  short-lived gains (a single car-free day; a hotspot count that fell this week), and research without
  recovery outcomes (a coral-spawning timing study). Research that MEASURES recovery was already IN.

This widens nature_recovery v4's STEP 1 (which required observed recovery or delivered in-force protection).
It binds the next prompt revision (nature_recovery v5, #71). It does not change the live filter by itself.
