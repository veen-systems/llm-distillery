## 4. Sport results — OUT, unless there is more

> A result alone is out; in only when the article reports a benefit to people beyond the result
> (e.g. a programme that got kids into sport).

A medal, a season's champions or a world record, reported as a result, is OUT.

## 5. Paywalled teasers — OUT

> If there's not enough text to show a process going well, it doesn't qualify.

A headline plus a line or two of teaser (paywall, Google News echo) is **OUT**, not `cannot_judge`.
`cannot_judge` stays only for text that is broken or not an article at all.
