# Two more Thriving scope rulings — sport results and paywalled teasers

**2026-09-25. Owner ruling, given in session**, after the adj3 band audit failed its drift check
(`docs/evidence/2026-09-25-adj3-band-audit/README.md`): two of its five judge disagreements were sport
results and one was a paywalled teaser, and neither had a ruling. These **add to**
`2026-09-24-thriving-scope-rulings.md` (rulings 1–3), which still stand.

⚠️ **How they were given.** As on 2026-09-24: the owner chose between options the assistant wrote.
The ruling is the option selected, in the wording below.

## 4. Sport results — OUT, unless there is more

> A result alone is out; in only when the article reports a benefit to people beyond the result
> (e.g. a programme that got kids into sport).

A medal, a season's champions or a world record, reported as a result, is OUT.

## 5. Paywalled teasers — OUT

> If there's not enough text to show a process going well, it doesn't qualify.

A headline plus a line or two of teaser (paywall, Google News echo) is **OUT**, not `cannot_judge`.
`cannot_judge` stays only for text that is broken or not an article at all.

## ⏸️ Open, NOT ruled: consumer-court compensation vs ruling 1

Ruling 1 says court rulings are IN when they deliver something to a named person, and names
*compensation* as an example. In the band audit's owner check (#5) the owner kept a consumer
commission's Rs 60k award to one bus passenger **OUT**. That contradicts ruling 1 as worded. It is
recorded here for the owner to settle; ruling 1 is **not** changed, and the judges still apply it.
