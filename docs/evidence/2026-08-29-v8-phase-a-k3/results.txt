DEVIATIONS from PREREGISTRATION.md, reported not substituted:
  1. The pre-registered rule was 'median between-arm |d| (k=3 mean) <= 1.5x median
     within-arm |d| (run pairs)'. It is unusable and biased, two ways. Biased: a k=3
     mean is less noisy by construction, so it favours 'parity holds'. Unusable: the
     within-arm median |d| is 0.000 in stratum R -- most rows are identical run to
     run -- so the ratio divides by zero. A bar that cannot be evaluated is not a
     bar; the fix is the matched pair-level test above, whose null is measured, not
     assumed.
  2. The pre-registered op-point rule ('between-arm count inside the range of the two
     within-arm counts') had no error bar: with counts of 3-8 out of 150, Poisson
     noise alone spans that range. Replaced by the same bootstrap.
