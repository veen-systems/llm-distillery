## Where the definition comes from

1. **Research** (both checked on 2026-10-02):
   - **Baumeister & Leary 1995**, "The need to belong", *Psychological Bulletin* 117(3):497–529. Belonging needs two
     things together: frequent, pleasant interactions with a few other people, and a stable, ongoing framework of
     concern for each other's welfare.
   - **McMillan & Chavis 1986**, "Sense of community". It has four elements: membership; influence; integration and
     fulfilment of needs; and a shared emotional connection that grows from shared history and time spent together.
2. **The filter's own definition:** belonging v1's STEP 1 (`filters/belonging/v1/prompt-compressed.md`). It covers
   lived belonging, which is what the six scored dimensions measure.
3. **The owner's rulings** (2026-10-02; marked *(ruled)* below).

## The question

**Is the relationship between specific people, or between people and their place, the story? And is it ongoing or
shared, rather than a one-off moment?**

**Check: remove the relationship.** If a story is still left, the article is not Belonging. What might be left:
- heroism or an achievement
- a policy, an innovation or a result
- a harm or a conflict
- a culture or history topic
- a ceremony

## In scope: lived belonging

The article shows specific people, now, in one of these:
- **Intergenerational ties:** grandparents and grandchildren, elders passing on knowledge, mentoring across ages.
  This includes one family passing a practice or craft down the generations (ruled). A family's one-off moment does
  not count.
- **Thick community bonds and mutual aid:** neighbours who know and help each other, a congregation, a village, a
  volunteer group that keeps meeting, or a social club whose members keep meeting, even when nothing is built (ruled;
  this replaces Q3's "clubs out"). Professional networking stays out, as v1 excludes it.
- **Place attachment:** families rooted for generations, people returning to a place, a community keeping its place
  alive.
- **Purpose through contribution:** ongoing service to one's own community.
- **Slow presence and rituals:** shared meals, festivals and traditions **the people themselves carry** (Q1, ruled),
  and unhurried time together.
- **Reciprocal care:** caring for family, elders or neighbours as an ongoing relationship. This includes
  **recognition of carers and helpers** (ruled).
- **Community events where people take part**, not events where they only watch (ruled).
- **Programmes where the participants build something together** (Q3, ruled). A service delivered by staff does not
  count.
- **A movement or protest, only when the article is about the community it built:** the people supporting each other,
  organising and sustaining one another (ruled). A protest's political fight or outcome is `out_harm_is_story`.
- **A volunteer movement told through its founder:** in only when the article shows the volunteers and their bonds.
  A founder's profile with totals is out (ruled).
- **After a harm:** a gathering counts when it does something together (rebuilding, protecting, caring). A gathering
  only to grieve or remember does not (Q2, ruled; Robinvale's protection counts).

**One person helping others** (ruled, split):
- In, when it is an ongoing relationship of care or contribution, e.g. a carer, a long-time neighbour, an elder who
  teaches.
- Out, when it is a one-off heroic act or a viral kindness (see `out_one_moment`).

## Out of scope: one verdict per class

| verdict | the shape |
|---|---|
| `out_gift_official` | an official ceremony, inauguration, state commemoration or campaign; a one-off gift, grant or award from an official, company, celebrity, foundation, or a group's one-off donation |
| `out_one_moment` | one person's or one family's one-off act, viral moment, achievement, talent or biography: a rescue, a stranger's kindness, a graduation, a record, a profile — including a portrait of one person at a shared event (ruled) |
| `out_harm_is_story` | the harm, grievance, conflict or demand is the story, including a protest's political outcome |
| `out_culture_topic` | culture, heritage, history or identity as a topic (a museum, book, film, artist, cuisine, history), with nobody living it now |
| `out_event_spectated` | an audience at a spectacle: a parade, a race, a show, a celebrity appearance |
| `out_other` | anything else off-lens: innovation, policy, business, animals, research, how-to, job ads, a service run by staff |
| `cannot_judge` | the text is too short or broken to tell what happened |

`in_scope` is not the default. If you cannot name the specific people, the relationship, and why it is ongoing or
shared, it is not `in_scope`.

## Not too wide

The owner ruled: *"we shouldn't make it too wide, otherwise we get a hose, like uplifting."* A warm, positive or
kind story is not Belonging by default. The test is the relationship, not the mood.
